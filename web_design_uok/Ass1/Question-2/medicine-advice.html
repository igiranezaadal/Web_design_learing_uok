Medicine Advice for Parents

Diarrhoea and dehydration

Mix 1 packet of ORS with clean water (H2O). Recommended by WHO.

ORS sachets price: 3,500 RWF 2,800 RWF now in stock

Give a 2nd dose if symptoms persist after the 1st hour.

Fever and dosing

Do not give this syrup to children under 2 years

Never double a missed dose.

For children < 15 kg & babies, use the syrup, not tablets.

Standard dosage is 500 mg twice daily. Avoid excess CO2 carbonated drinks during treatment.

"Please ensure all medicines are kept safely out of reach of young children."
— Head Nurse
