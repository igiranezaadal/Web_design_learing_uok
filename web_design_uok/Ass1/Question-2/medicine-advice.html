Medicine Advice for Parents

Do not give this syrup to children under 2 years.

If your child misses a dose, give it as soon as you remember. Never double a missed dose.

Diarrhoea and dehydration

Mix one sachet of ORS in one litre of clean H2O (boiled water, cooled). Give small sips every few minutes.

Box of 20 ORS sachets: 3,500 RWF 2,800 RWF – now in stock

Fever and dosing

Take the 2nd dose 8 hours after the 1st. Each tablet contains 500 mg of paracetamol.

For children < 15 kg & babies, use the syrup, not tablets.

A room full of smoke adds CO2 and makes breathing harder: keep the child near fresh air.

Most fevers in young children are not dangerous, but a child who refuses to drink must be brought to us the same day.

— Head Nurse, Nyagatare Health Centre

The WHO says: Clean water and hand washing prevent most diarrhoea.
